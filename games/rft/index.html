HOW TO PLAY

Services make files by themselves. Buy more to make more. Each file sells to the company that wants that kind of file, at a price that moves with its stock.

Hardware decides how much your PC can run. Over the CPU limit, everything slows down. Run hot and it throttles. RAM caps how many services you can have. Storage sets how long your PC keeps working while you're away. The network card makes files sell for more.

Processing makes files worth more. Contracts pay well for a batch of one kind of file, if you deliver in time.

Reformat wipes your services, hardware and Polygons for Format Points. Every point makes everything produce more, forever.

Log in with Discord and you can cash out: ⬡5 for every ⬡1,000 you earn here goes into your RendConomy wallet. Achievements here unlock in the bot too.
