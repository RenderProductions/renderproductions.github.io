PRIVACY POLICY

Render's Vision, the Rend-Conomy bot. Last updated: November 10, 2026

This policy explains what information the Render's Vision Discord bot, which runs the Rend-Conomy game ("the bot"), collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The bot is run by Render Productions, the team behind The Render Room Discord server.

What the bot receives and stores

  • Discord account details: your Discord user ID and username, which Discord provides whenever you use a command.
  • Server details: the ID of the server and channel where a command is used.
  • Commands: the bot receives messages in servers it is in so it can detect commands. It acts on messages that start with its command prefix (r!) and on its slash commands. During an r!work shift, it also reads your next message in that channel as your answer to the task. We do not store the content of messages.
  • Game data: your fictional cash balance, any debt to TODD, the shares and items you hold, your job and shifts, your custom title, your trades, cooldown times, loans with other players, and any player company you start. This is tied to your Discord user ID and the server you play in.

How the information is used

  • To run the stock market game: processing your trades, balances, and rewards.
  • To reply to your commands, and to send receipts for your trades, purchases, and loans to your direct messages (you can turn these off with r!receipts off).
  • To keep the bot fair and working, including spotting abuse and fixing bugs.

Player companies are public

If you start a player company, its name, ticker, level, treasury, share price, number of employees, and your Discord username are shown publicly on the Exchange page and in a public data file in this website's GitHub repository. Company names and usernames also appear in the bot's replies in any server where it's used.

Public profiles and logging in

The bot publishes a public profile for each player on the profile page and in a public data file in this website's GitHub repository: your Discord ID, name and profile picture, your achievements, collectibles, title, net worth, job, shifts worked and best casino win, and the names of the servers where you play. You can hide yours at any time with r!profile private, and it disappears the next time the site updates (within a few minutes).

"Log in with Discord" on the site only asks Discord who you are (your ID, name and picture) so it can show your profile. That happens entirely in your browser: the site has no server, nothing is sent to us, and the short-lived login token is used once and thrown away. The site remembers who you are in your browser until you log out.

Where it is stored

The bot runs on a server from Bot-Hosting.net, where game data is stored in a database with regular backups. Player company information is also published to GitHub, as described above. Market prices come from a public file on this website and contain no personal information. Game data from the bot's earlier version on Bot Designer for Discord (BDFD) was not carried over.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. It is only handled by the services needed to run the bot (Discord, Bot-Hosting.net, and GitHub), or shared if required by law.

How long we keep it

Game data is kept for as long as the bot is in use, so your balance and holdings carry over. We may reset game data, for example for a new season or after a bug fix.

Your choices

  • You can stop using the bot at any time.
  • Server administrators can remove the bot from their server whenever they like.
  • You can ask us to delete or reset your game data. Contact us using the details below.

Children

Discord requires users to be at least 13 years old, and the bot is not meant for anyone younger. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed, and continuing to use the bot after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Join The Render Room on Discord and message a moderator with any privacy questions or deletion requests.
