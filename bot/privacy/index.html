PRIVACY POLICY

Rend-Conomy Bot. Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy explains what information the Rend-Conomy Discord bot ("the bot") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The bot is run by Render Productions, the team behind The Render Room Discord server.

What the bot receives and stores

  • Discord account details: your Discord user ID and username, which Discord provides whenever you use a command.
  • Server details: the ID of the server and channel where a command is used.
  • Commands and AI prompts: the bot receives messages in servers it is in so it can detect commands. It only acts on messages that start with its command prefix (r!) or that you send to its AI features. We do not intentionally store the content of other messages.
  • Game data: your fictional cash balance, the shares you hold, and the time you last claimed a daily reward. This is tied to your Discord user ID.

How the information is used

  • To run the stock market game: processing your trades, balances, and rewards.
  • To reply to your commands and prompts.
  • To keep the bot fair and working, including spotting abuse and fixing bugs.

AI features

Text you send to the bot's AI features is passed to a third-party AI service so it can generate a reply. Do not send personal, private, or sensitive information to the bot. AI replies can be inaccurate.

Where it is stored

The bot is built and hosted with Bot Designer for Discord (BDFD), so game data is stored on BDFD's systems and is also subject to BDFD's own terms and policies. Market prices come from a public file on this website and contain no personal information.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. It is only handled by the services needed to run the bot (Discord, BDFD, and the AI service described above), or shared if required by law.

How long we keep it

Game data is kept for as long as the bot is in use, so your balance and holdings carry over. We may reset game data, for example for a new season or after a bug fix.

Your choices

  • You can stop using the bot at any time.
  • Server administrators can remove the bot from their server whenever they like.
  • You can ask us to delete or reset your game data. Contact us using the details below.

Children

Discord requires users to be at least 13 years old, and the bot is not meant for anyone younger. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed, and continuing to use the bot after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Join The Render Room on Discord and message a moderator with any privacy questions or deletion requests.
