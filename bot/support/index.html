SUPPORT RENDCONOMY

A tip jar, not a store.

RendConomy is free to add and free to play, and it's going to stay that way. Keeping it online does cost money every month, so if you enjoy it and want to help, you can leave a tip.

What tips pay for

  • The bot's hosting: the server that runs RendConomy around the clock.
  • The site's backend: the Cloudflare service behind logging in, the Play hub, live prices, the Trading Floor and Story of Varandia campaigns.
  • Anything left over goes toward future updates.

What tips don't do

Tips buy nothing in the bot. No Polygons, items, collectibles, titles, perks, boosts or advantages, and there never will be. Everyone plays the same game whether they've tipped or not. Real money and the game's Polygons are kept completely apart.

Other ways to help

  • Add the bot to your server: the bot page has the invite.
  • Tell us what to build next, or what's broken, in The Render Room.
  • Vote for the wiki's Featured page with r!wiki vote.

Thank you for playing.
