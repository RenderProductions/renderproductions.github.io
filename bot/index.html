REND-CONOMY
BOT

A Discord bot with a fictional stock market, daily rewards, and built-in AI features. Add it to your server in a couple of clicks.

Add to your server Join the Render Room

What it does

  • Stock market

    Buy and sell shares in four fictional companies. Prices move every few minutes, and everything uses fake money. Trading is open 9 AM to 9 PM Eastern, Monday to Saturday.

    TRADING

  • Portfolio

    Check your cash, the shares you own, and your total net worth at any time.

    TRACKING

  • Daily rewards

    Claim free Polygons once a day to keep your portfolio growing.

    REWARDS

  • AI features

    The bot can use AI to generate replies. AI answers can be wrong, so don't rely on them for anything important.

    AI

Commands

Prefix: r!
Command What it does
r!marketShows current prices and 24-hour changes.
r!buy <ticker> <amount>Buys shares, for example r!buy RRM 5.
r!sell <ticker> <amount or all>Sells shares you own.
r!chart <ticker>Shows a price chart for one company, for example r!chart BLU.
r!portfolioShows your cash, shares, and net worth.
r!dailyClaims your free daily Polygons.
r!helpLists the commands in Discord.

Live prices are on the Rend-Conomy market page.

Adding the bot

Use the button above and pick the server you want. You need the Manage Server permission in that server to add a bot.
