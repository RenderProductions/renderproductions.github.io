REND-CONOMY
BOT

A Discord bot with a fictional stock market, company jobs, a shop, daily rewards, and built-in AI features. Add it to your server in a couple of clicks.

Add to your server Join the Render Room

What it does

  • Stock market

    Buy and sell shares in four fictional companies. Prices move every few minutes, and everything uses fake money. Trading is open 3 AM to 9 PM Eastern, every day.

    TRADING

  • Jobs

    Work for one of the four companies with quick in-chat tasks every 45 minutes. Pay follows your company's stock price, and loyal workers get promoted all the way to Executive.

    EARNING

  • Shop

    Spend Polygons on coffee, overtime passes, mystery boxes, and custom titles. Stock is shared across every server and restocks hourly, so the good stuff goes fast.

    SPENDING

  • Dividends

    Every share pays a daily dividend, and smaller companies pay more: BNW 2.5%, NACH 1.5%, BLU 1%, RRM 0.5% of the share price. Unclaimed dividends keep piling up. Goal news matters too: a failure freezes payouts for 24 hours, and a success pays a bonus.

    INCOME

  • TODD Loans

    Lost it all? TODD will bail you out with 500 Polygons, for a price. Pay him back before you go near the slots again. He'll remember. (Mostly.)

    DEBT

  • Portfolio

    Check your cash, the shares you own, and your total net worth at any time.

    TRACKING

  • Daily rewards

    Claim free Polygons once a day to keep your portfolio growing.

    REWARDS

  • AI features

    The bot can use AI to generate replies. AI answers can be wrong, so don't rely on them for anything important.

    AI

Commands

Prefix: r!
Command What it does
MARKET
r!marketShows current prices and 24-hour changes.
r!buy <ticker> <amount>Buys shares, for example r!buy RRM 5.
r!sell <ticker> <amount or all>Sells shares you own.
r!chart <ticker>Shows a price chart for one company, for example r!chart BLU.
r!portfolio [@user]Shows your cash, shares, debt, net worth, and title. Mention someone to see theirs.
r!dailyClaims your free daily Polygons.
r!dividendsCollects the dividends your shares have earned. Shares you buy start earning after your next claim.
JOBS
r!apply <ticker>Joins a company as an Intern, for example r!apply NACH. You can work for one company at a time.
r!workStarts a shift. Answer the quick task within 20 seconds for full pay. Wrong or slow answers still earn 40%. Every 45 minutes.
r!quitLeaves your job. Locked for 24 hours after you're hired, and your rank resets.
SHOP
r!shopShows items, prices, stock left, and the next restock.
r!purchase <item>Buys an item: coffee, overtime, mystery, resume, or title. One of each per restock.
r!settitle <text>Sets the custom title you bought (32 characters max).
TODD
r!bailoutBroke? If your net worth is under 100, TODD lends you 500 Polygons. You owe him 600.
r!repay <amount or all>Pays TODD back. While you owe him anything, r!slots is off limits.
GAMES OF CHANCE
r!slotsTry your luck at the slot machine. Not available while you owe TODD.
AI
r!setaiSets the AI's personality in its replies to you. Each user has their own.
r!aiTalks to the AI using your chosen personality. Don't share private or sensitive information.
OTHER
r!helpLists the commands in Discord.
r!cleartitle @userModerators only. Removes someone's custom title.

Live prices are on the Rend-Conomy market page, and each company has its own page: RRM, NACH, BLU, BNW. New commands show up in the patch notes.
