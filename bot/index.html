About me

A fictional stock market for Discord. Trade shares, work a job, shop, borrow from TODD when you're broke, and start your own company that anyone can invest in. Everything runs on fake money called Polygons.

What it does

  • Stock market

    Buy and sell shares in five fictional companies. Prices move every few minutes, and everything uses fake money. Trading is open 3 AM to 9 PM Eastern, every day.

    TRADING

  • Jobs

    Work for one of the five companies with quick in-chat tasks every 15 minutes: mod queues, memory orders, audits, bug hunts and more. Pay follows your company's stock price, fast answers earn tips, and a performance review every 10 shifts decides who gets promoted.

    EARNING

  • Shop

    Spend Polygons on coffee, overtime passes, mystery boxes, and custom titles. Stock is shared across every server and restocks hourly, so the good stuff goes fast.

    SPENDING

  • Dividends

    Every share pays a daily dividend, and smaller companies pay more: BSTG 3%, BNW 2.5%, NACH 1.5%, BLU 1%, RRM 0.5% of the share price. Bastardly Glory pays the most because it's the biggest gamble: 3% of a soaring stock is a lot, and 3% of a crashed one is not. Unclaimed dividends keep piling up. Each company's board sets how much of that it actually pays: anywhere from 125% down to a full suspension, reviewed every 8 to 16 hours depending on how the company is doing. A failed goal cuts the rate, a success restores it (a big one boosts it to 150% for a while), and good news sometimes pays a special dividend. The bot and the company pages show each dividend's current rate.

    INCOME

  • TODD Loans

    Lost it all? TODD will bail you out with ⬡ 500, for a price. Pay him back before you go near the slots again. He'll remember. (Mostly.)

    DEBT

  • Portfolio

    Check your cash, the shares you own, and your total net worth at any time.

    TRACKING

  • Daily rewards

    Claim free Polygons once a day to keep your portfolio growing.

    REWARDS

Commands

Prefix: r! · or use / slash commands
Command What it does
MARKET
r!marketShows current prices and 24-hour changes.
r!buy <ticker> <amount>Buys shares, for example r!buy RRM 5.
r!sell <ticker> <amount or all>Sells shares you own.
r!chart <ticker>Shows a price chart for one company, for example r!chart BLU.
r!compare <ticker> <ticker>Puts two companies side by side, for example r!compare RRM BLU.
r!balance [@user]Shows your cash, shares, debt, net worth, and title. Mention someone to see theirs. r!bal and r!portfolio work too.
r!dailyClaims your free daily Polygons. Resets at midnight Eastern.
r!reserveThe house isn't infinite. Shows the Polygon Reserve every payout comes from. Under 25%, payouts shrink. At zero, the market crashes.
r!dividendsCollects the dividends your shares have earned. Shares you buy start earning after your next claim.
JOBS
r!apply <ticker>Joins a company, for example r!apply NACH. You can work for one company at a time. Nobody hires during a crash, and bankrupt companies can't hire at all.
r!workStarts a shift. Answer the task within 20 seconds for full pay, and fast answers earn a speed tip on top. Wrong or slow answers still earn 40%. Coworkers working in the same channel get a team bonus. Every 15 minutes.
r!jobYour rank, next performance review, accuracy, earnings and cooldown, plus what's happening at your company. r!job remind on DMs you when your next shift is ready.
r!gigNo job? Quick freelance tasks for a little pay, every 10 minutes.
r!quitLeaves your job. Locked for 24 hours after you're hired. Your rank there is saved if you come back.
SHOP
r!shopShows items, prices, stock left, and the next restock.
r!purchase <item>Buys an item into your inventory: coffee, overtime, mystery, resume, or title. One of each per restock.
r!inventory [@user]Shows the items you own. r!inv works too.
r!use <item>Uses an item when you're ready, like r!use coffee or opening a mystery box.
r!settitle <text>Uses a Title from your inventory to set your custom title (32 characters max).
YOUR OWN COMPANY
r!found <ticker> <name>Starts your own company for ⬡ 2,500. A mod approves the name first; if it's rejected you get your money back.
r!company [ticker]Shows a player company, or yours if you leave out the ticker.
r!exchangeLists the player-run companies. Also on the Exchange page.
r!invest <amount>Puts your Polygons into your company's treasury.
r!upgradeLevels your company up: Startup, Small Business, Firm, Corporation, Conglomerate. Paid from the treasury.
r!ipoTakes a Firm or bigger public, so anyone (except you) can buy and sell its shares.
r!salary <amount or max>Pays you up to 10% of a public company's treasury once a day. The stock dips when you do.
LOANS
r!loan @player <amount> <interest>Asks another player for a loan, for example r!loan @KorbedOut 1000 4 (4% a day, up to 25%). They accept or decline with buttons.
r!payback @player <amount or all>Pays a loan back early. Interest grows every day for 7 days; after that, half of everything you earn goes to the lender until it's paid.
r!loansShows what you owe and what you're owed.
r!forgive @playerWipes out a loan someone owes you.
TODD
r!bailoutBroke? If your net worth is under ⬢ 100, TODD lends you ⬡ 500. You owe him ⬡ 600.
r!repay <amount or all>Pays TODD back. While you owe him anything, r!slots is off limits.
GAMES OF CHANCE
r!slots <amount>Try your luck at the slot machine. Bets from ⬡ 10 to ⬡ 1,000. The whole Rend-Sino is off limits while you owe TODD.
r!coinflip <amount> heads|tailsCall it right for 1.95x.
r!dice <amount> over|under <n>Roll 1 to 100. The riskier your number, the bigger the payout.
r!blackjack <amount>Blackjack against TODD with Hit, Stand and Double buttons. Blackjack pays 2.5x.
r!bus <amount>Ride the Bus: red or black, higher or lower, inside or outside, then the suit. Every button shows what it pays, and you can cash out between rounds.
r!hilo <amount>Higher or Lower: build a streak, and cash out before you call one wrong.
r!mines <amount> [bombs]Minesweeper on a 4×4 field. Every safe tile raises your multiplier. More bombs, bigger payouts.
r!rocket <amount>The multiplier climbs live until the rocket crashes. Cash out before it does.
r!derby <horse> <amount>The Polygon Derby. The first bet opens 45 seconds of betting for the whole channel, then the race runs live.
r!lottery [buy <tickets>]The weekly lottery. Tickets are ⬡ 50, up to 20 per draw, and the pot is drawn Sunday at 8 PM Eastern.
r!gamblingYour record at every game: played, won or lost, and your biggest hit.
OTHER
r!helpLists the commands in Discord.
r!receipts <on or off>Receipts for trades, shop purchases, loans and company money are sent to your DMs. Turn them off any time.
r!cleartitle @userModerators only. Removes someone's custom title.
r!review · r!approve · r!rejectRender Room mods only. Approves or rejects new player companies.

Live prices are on the Rend-Conomy market page, and each company has its own page: RRM, NACH, BLU, BNW, BSTG. New commands show up in the patch notes.
