Classes

Your class is what you do. In Varandia a class adds to your Health every level, points at the stats it leans on, and makes the spells on its list easier: their Need drops by 1. Anyone can still learn any spell.

    Reading D&D features

    Spell slots don't exist in Varandia. Wherever a feature talks about slots, you pay the spell's Stamina instead.

    Proficiency bonus works as in D&D: +2 at level 1. Add it to rolls for anything you're proficient in.
