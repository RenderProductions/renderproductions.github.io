The Spellbook

Cast with d20 + half Intelligence; meet or beat the Need. A spell costs 1 Stamina as a cantrip and 2 per level after that. Roll under it and the spell just doesn't fire (the Stamina is still spent). A natural 1 always fails and costs you 1d4 health per level. Chaotic spells start 2 harder, get easier as you level, and can surge.
