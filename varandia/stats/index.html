Stats & Dice

The seven stats

Making a character

  1. Roll a d6 for every stat. That's where each one starts.
  2. Spend 20 points however you like. There's no cap: put them all in one stat if you want.
  3. Add your race. Its modifiers go on top, and can take a stat below zero.

Rolling

Roll a d20 and add half the stat, rounded down. Meet or beat the number the GM sets and you succeed.

Only half counts so that a huge stat still has to roll. The full number stays on your sheet for everything else, like how much you can lift.

Levelling up

You level up when the GM says so, after the big moments of the story, up to level 20. Each level:

  • +1 point to put in any stat.
  • Training: every stat with a training mark grows by 1, then the marks clear. A natural 20 marks the stat you rolled; the GM can mark one after a hard roll.
  • More Health and Stamina, a bigger proficiency bonus every four levels, Chaotic spells get easier every two, and new class features.
