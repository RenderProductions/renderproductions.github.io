Chapter IV

Magic

Anyone can use magic, but it takes a toll on their Stamina.

All magic falls under one of two umbrellas.

Chaotic magic

The wild, raw energy that gives Varandia its life, cast as it is, with no element. Pure, powerful and unpredictable. The higher your level, the easier it becomes to use.

Spells like Counterspell, Teleport and Magic Missile. See them all.

Controlled magic

Chaotic magic that has been trained into an element and harnessed into runes and magical artifacts: wands, staves, tomes, crystal balls and the like. Every element is Controlled magic, Evil included.

Learning spells

You learn an elemental spell by harnessing Chaotic magic and training it into one element. That's where the standard spells come from: fireball and the rest.

Miss the Need and the spell doesn't fire. Only a natural 1 costs you health.
