Chapter III

Magic

Anyone can use magic, but it takes a toll on their Stamina.

Chaotic magic

The wild, raw energy that gives Varandia its life. Pure, powerful, and unpredictable.

Controlled magic

Magic harnessed into runes and magical artifacts: wands, staves, tomes, crystal balls and the like.

Learning spells

You learn a spell by harnessing Chaotic magic and training it into a certain kind of Controlled magic. That's where the standard spells come from: fireball and the rest.

The higher your level, the easier Chaotic magic becomes to use.

Fail a magic roll and it costs you some health.
