The Rend-Conomy is the economy game inside Render Vision, the Render Productions Discord bot. You earn Polygons (written ⬡), spend them, invest them, and occasionally lose them in spectacular ways. Every server has its own economy: your balance in one server has nothing to do with another. Your achievements follow you everywhere.

Joining

There's nothing to sign up for. The first command you use creates your account with ⬡500. Every command works as r!command or as a slash command (/command); r!help lists them by section, and the commands page lists every one.

Your first ten minutes

  1. Claim your daily: r!daily pays ⬡250 once per day (Eastern time). Keep a streak going for the achievements.
  2. Get a job: r!apply RRM (or NACH, BLU, BNW, BSTG). Then r!work every 15 minutes. Each shift gives you a quick task; type the answer in the channel. See Work.
  3. Do a gig between shifts: r!gig needs no job and works every 10 minutes. r!oddjob gives you up to 10 more small jobs a day.
  4. Look around the shop: r!shop opens Marquez's shelves. A Fishing Rod or some seeds are good first buys.
  5. Check your money: r!bal shows everything you have, what you owe, and your net worth.
TIPJust talking in the server earns Polygons too: a few for each message (one a minute counts), up to ⬡300 a day. Spamming doesn't help.

Where Polygons come from

Almost every payout in the game, from shifts to rent to casino wins, is paid out of the Polygon Reserve, a single pot shared by everyone. Almost everything you spend goes back into it. That's worth understanding early, because it's the reason payouts can shrink.

If you go broke

TODD will lend you ⬡500 with r!bailout when you have less than ⬡100, and you'll owe him ⬡600. While you owe TODD, the Rend-Sino won't let you gamble. Pay him back with r!repay.

Where to go next

Read about the Reserve, then pick a path. Achievements are a great guide to what's possible: r!achievements shows yours, and every one pays out the first time.
