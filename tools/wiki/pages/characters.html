The Rend-Conomy has a narrator, and it has a cast. These are the ones you'll meet.

Marquez

Marquez runs the shop. He's reserved, kind and a little old-fashioned, and he only ever calls you friend. He stocks everything from coffee to fishing rods to seeds, buys your fish, crops and Goods, and sets out one collectible a week. He doesn't talk about Mr. Glaz.

Spend enough at his counter and he'll remember you: his loyalty card goes from Stranger to Family, and Family gets the back room. See Main Street.

Where: r!shop, r!sellfish, r!sellcrops.

Chuck Alfrey

Chuck runs the Bait & Tackle down at the docks: an old fisherman in a yellow raincoat with a story for everything, and maybe a third of them are true. He's talkative, superstitious (never whistle on the pier), swears he once saw the White Whale, keeps the server's records in chalk, and talks to the fish. He and Marquez are old friends. He'll call you Greenhorn until you've filled a page of the Fishdex, then Deckhand, Skipper and finally Old Salt. He sells the rods, the Cooler and the Boat, and pays you when you finish a page of the Fishdex.

Where: r!fish shop, or Chuck's on Main Street.

TODD

TODD is a monitor with opinions. He's the house at the Rend-Sino, the lender of last resort (r!bailout), the law when the Underground gets out of hand, and the one who drops into chat with a question and a pot of Polygons. Win ten in a row at his casino and he'll have you escorted out. Ask him to rate something with r!rate; he'll do it badly. The server's first landmark is a statue of him.

Mr. Glaz

A red robe with the hood pulled over his face, and in its shadow a single white eye. Paranoid to the point of madness: he whispers, then snaps, counts things nobody else can see, and never says anyone's name. He only comes out at night. He keeps his rules. See the Underground.
