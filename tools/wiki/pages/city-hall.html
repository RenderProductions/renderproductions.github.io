Every server elects a mayor each week. The mayor has real power over the server's economy: they can stop a company from being crashed and swallowed, set taxes, fund public works, and issue decrees. Everything they do goes on a public record, and the server can vote them out.

Elections

  • Nominations run Monday to Thursday: r!mayor run [slogan]. You can drop out with r!mayor withdraw.
  • Voting runs Friday to Sunday at 9 PM Eastern: r!vote @player. Anyone with an account in the server can vote, once; you can change your vote until it closes. r!mayor race shows the ballot.
  • Results come in Sunday at 9 PM Eastern. The winner is mayor until the next Sunday at 9 PM. A tie goes to whoever signed up first. If nobody runs, the seat stays empty for the week.
  • Who can run: active players: you have to have played on at least ? different days in the last 7, with an account at least ? days old in the server. r!mayor eligible checks.
  • Term limit: at most ? terms in a row; then sit one out before running again.

The mayor's powers

r!mayor help lists them in Discord. Every action needs a reason, and it goes on the public record (r!mayor log).

Business

  • Block a takeover bid or merger: r!mayor block PREY <reason>. A blocked bidder gets their premium back.
  • Freeze a player company's trading for 24 hours: nobody can buy or sell its shares. ? a term.
  • Investigate a player: their buys and sells in player companies go on the public record for ? days.
  • Fine a company (from its treasury) or a player (from their wallet): up to ?, at most ?, ? a term. Fines go into the city treasury.
  • Bail out a company with a grant from the city treasury, to save one that's being crashed.

Taxes

  • A sales tax on Marquez's shop (0 to ?), a property tax on top of the building tax (0 to ? of a building's value a day), and a tax on casino winnings (0 to ? of what you win over your bet). r!mayor tax shop 5.
  • Changes start at midnight Eastern, so nobody gets ambushed. Taxes belong to the mayor's term: a new mayor starts at 0.
  • Collections: the mayor sets Collect Call's fee (? to ?) and grace period (? to ? days). The fee goes to the city.

Public works

The mayor designs them: pick an effect, a size, how long, and a name, and the city treasury pays. r!mayor build rent 10 3 Main Street Repaving; r!mayor quote rent 10 3 prices it first. One of each effect at a time.

The cost is the rate × the size × the days × the server's active players (at least 5).

Decrees and appointments

  • Decrees: anything the mayor wants to say goes on the record (r!mayor decree <text>). Once a day a decree can also do something: Happy Hour for 2 hours, no heists for 24 hours, double dailies until midnight, a market holiday for player companies until midnight, or an amnesty (everyone's Heat drops by 25).
  • Appointments: a Treasurer (funds public works and bailouts) and a Police Chief (investigates and freezes). r!mayor appoint chief @player.

The limits

  • The mayor and the appointees can never pay themselves, or anything they own, from the city treasury.
  • Conflicts of interest: no business powers on a company they own, hold shares in, or are bidding on (or the company it's merging with).
  • Everything goes on the public record, with a reason.
  • Impeachment: r!impeach opens a 24-hour vote. If more than half as many players as voted in the last election vote yes (and at least ?), the mayor is out; their taxes and decrees end, and the seat is empty until the next election. One impeachment vote a term.

The city treasury

Taxes, fines and collection fees go in; public works and bailouts come out. It's public: r!mayor shows the balance, the taxes, what's being built and the latest entries on the record. Unspent money stays for the next mayor.
