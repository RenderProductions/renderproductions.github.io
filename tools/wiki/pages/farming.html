Every server gives you a small farm. Buy seeds from Marquez (or order them wholesale, if you own a Warehouse), plant them, water them once per stage, and harvest when they're ready. Crops grow in real time, even while you're away. Sell your basket to Marquez, at the farmers' market, or by freight.

Crops

r!plant carrot fills every empty plot (or r!plant corn 2 for just plot 2); then r!water and r!harvest. Tomatoes keep producing: after the first harvest they regrow twice, in half the time. Pumpkins are worth 25% more in October.

TIPCrows can eat a crop (a Scarecrow keeps them off), and Storm events can flatten crops outside a Greenhouse. Rain waters everything for free.

Quality grades

Every crop is graded the moment it's harvested. Its grade sets the range its price is rolled from when you sell it: each one is rolled separately, so a basket of the same crop sells for a little more or less every time.

Harvest a Prime crop for Quality Control, and a Prime crop that's also Golden or Rainbow for Cream of the Crop.

Daily prices

On top of grades, every crop's price moves each day, between 80% and 120% of normal, the same for everyone. r!farm prices shows today's. A Warehouse on the city map lets you sell at the best price of the last 3 days (5 at level 2, 7 at level 3).

Selling

  • To Marquez: r!sellcrops sells your whole basket.
  • At the farmers' market: r!sellcrops TICK sells to a player company for 10% more, and the company earns a cut (not your own company).
  • By freight: with a Station on the city map, ship the basket for 15% over market. See freight.

More plots and the Barn

Buy plots one at a time with r!buyplot. Past nine, you need a Barn on the city map, which unlocks six more. The farm always shows three rows: three plots across, or five with a Barn.

Fertilizer

Fertilizer makes a crop grow 50% faster and come out Prime more often. r!fertilize works it into every growing crop at once, as far as your Fertilizer goes, or r!fertilize 3 for one plot. Buy it from Marquez or make it from fishing junk with r!compost.

The farmhand

Hire him with r!farmhand hire for ⬡150 a day. While you're away he waters everything, harvests each crop the moment it's ready, and replants from his seed shed (the same crop as before, or what he has most of). He never buys seeds: stock his shed with r!farmhand give carrot 20.

  • Give him Fertilizer too (r!farmhand give fertilizer 10) and he works one into every crop he plants.
  • When you open your farm, he tells you what he did while you were gone. He DMs you when he runs out of seeds (turn that off with r!farmhand dms off).
  • If you can't pay him, he quits and leaves the shed with you. He doesn't sell your crops; that's still your call.
