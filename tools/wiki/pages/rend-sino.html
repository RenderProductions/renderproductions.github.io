The games

Plus three that work differently:

  • Ride the Bus (r!bus): four card guesses in a row, cash out any time after the first.
  • The Polygon Derby (r!derby): start a race, everyone in the channel bets on a horse, and it runs live.
  • The lottery (r!lottery): tickets are ⬡50 (up to 20 a week), and the pot is drawn every Sunday at 8 PM Eastern.

Lose a spin on Slots and you get Redeem yourself: one free spin of 15 reels, and seven of a kind wins your bet back.

The progressive jackpot

Every bet in the server adds 1% to the jackpot (3% during Happy Hour). Three 💎 on Slots win it: all of it at the maximum Slots bet, a share of it below (bet half the max, win half the jackpot). Then it starts over from ⬡10,000. r!jackpot shows how big it is.

VIP and free spins

VIP levels come from everything you've ever bet in the server. Each level comes with a free Slots spin every day (r!vip spin): win and it's paid, lose and it cost nothing. Free spins can hit the jackpot too.

The blackjack table

r!table 100 opens a table in the channel. Up to five players press Sit down, the host presses Deal, and everyone pays the stake. Hands are played in turn with Hit, Stand and Double; each player has 45 seconds before TODD stands for them. Then TODD plays his hand and every seat is paid like solo blackjack. Win at a table of three or more for House Party.

Happy Hour

A world event that comes around now and then: the jackpot grows three times as fast, VIPs get a second free spin, and TODD looks the other way on hot streaks.

TODD's rules

  • Hot streaks: win 10 games in a row and TODD has you escorted out for 3 hours. A Fake ID from the Connector gets you back in.
  • No gambling while you owe TODD for a bailout.
  • A game left unfinished for two minutes is forfeited. One moment between games.
