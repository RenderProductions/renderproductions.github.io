Any player can found a company. It has a treasury, levels up as you invest, and at level 3 it can go public: other players can buy its shares, and it gets its own page on the exchange.

Founding one

r!found TICK Your Company Name sends it to the server's moderators for approval; if the name is rejected you get your money back. Once approved, that money becomes the company's treasury. See it with r!company.

The treasury grows when you invest (r!invest), when players work there (every shift adds half the worker's pay, and anyone can r!apply TICK), and from share sales after it goes public.

Levels

Upgrades are paid from the treasury (r!upgrade). Every level costs upkeep each day, and raises what the company can pay.

Going public

At level 3 (Firm), r!ipo lists the company. Anyone but the owner can then buy and sell its shares, and the first 400 shares sold fund the company. Its price moves with trading, and its treasury earns from revenue: farmers' market sales, a Corner Shop's Storefront on the city map, and more. You can pay yourself a salary of up to 10% of the treasury a day (r!salary), but the stock dips when you do.

Upkeep and closing

The treasury pays upkeep every day. Miss a payment and the market panics; keep missing them and the price slides to zero and the company closes for good, taking every share with it. A private company closes after missing upkeep three days in a row.

Takeovers and mergers

  • Hostile takeover: a player who holds at least half of the shares for sale can bid for your company with r!takeover, putting up a premium of a quarter of its worth. You have a day to answer; no answer and it goes through.
  • Poison pill: defend for a tenth of the company's worth (r!takeover defend). The bidder gets their premium back minus a 20% break fee, which goes to you, and your company is shielded from takeovers for 7 days.
  • Merger: two owners can agree to merge with r!merge.
