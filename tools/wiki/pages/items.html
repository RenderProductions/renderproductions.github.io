Each server has its own stock, filled by Marquez's delivery truck three times a day, and prices move with demand (see Main Street). The prices below are list prices. One collectible is on sale each week, one per customer. Buy with r!shop buy <item> [amount] and use with r!use <item> [amount]; items marked "works on its own" do their thing just by being in your inventory.

  • Using lots at once: r!use mystery 10 or r!use mystery all opens up to ? in one message. Coffee, the Customizer Kit, the Megaphone, paints and the Rush Order go one at a time.
  • Mystery: ? of the time it holds ?, ? an Overtime pass, and otherwise ⬡?–?. On average that's about what it costs.
  • Rush Order: r!use rush order coffee has Marquez restock that one item to full, for everyone in your server. Not for collectibles, and you keep the Rush Order if the item is already full.
  • Selling back: r!shop sell <item> [amount|all]. Marquez pays ? of his list price and puts it back on his shelf. He doesn't buy collectibles (trade those with r!trade), and fish and crops have their own buyers.
  • Throwing away: r!discard <item> [amount|all], for nothing.
