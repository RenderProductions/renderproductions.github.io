The Polygon Reserve is the house's money. Shifts, gigs, rent, dividends, fish sales, casino wins and achievement payouts all come out of it. Shop purchases, bets, upkeep, fees and fines go back in. It's shared by every server, so how everyone plays affects everyone.

Lowest payouts10%

When payouts shrink

While the Reserve holds more than a quarter of its full size, every payout is paid in full. Below that, payouts shrink in step with it: at an eighth full, you get half; they never drop below a tenth. Your r!bal and most payout messages say when this is happening, and r!reserve shows exactly how full it is.

WHYA shrinking payout is the economy's brake: it stops the house from paying out money it doesn't have.

Helping it

  • Spending puts Polygons back: the shop, upkeep, bets, the farmhand's wage.
  • Donations: r!reserve donate <amount>. The top five patrons are listed in r!reserve, and there are achievements for it.
  • Landmarks: money donated to a landmark goes into the Reserve too.

Hurting it

A heist on the Reserve takes straight from it, so robbing it really does cost everyone. Big casino wins and achievement payouts drain it too, which is why every game's odds are published and fair rather than generous.
