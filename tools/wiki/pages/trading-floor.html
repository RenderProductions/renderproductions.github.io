The Trading Floor is RendConomy's Discord Activity: open it from the Activities button in a voice channel (or the app launcher) and the Rend-Conomy opens inside Discord. Your friends can see you're on the floor in your Discord status.

What's on it

  • The left side switches screens: the Market (pick a company to see its chart and trade it), the Rend-Sino, your Farm, your Desk and the Lake.
  • The right side is a command line: type any command (without r!) and the reply shows up with its buttons, the same as in chat. Games with buttons (blackjack, mines, the Rocket, heists, the Derby) work here too.

It runs as you, in the server you opened it in. On phones, it needs the server's Activities turned on for mobile.
