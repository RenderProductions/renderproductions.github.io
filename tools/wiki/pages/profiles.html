Every player has a public profile on the profile page: their achievements, collectibles, title, net worth, job and best Rend-Sino hit, across every server they play in.

Making it yours

Open your own profile while logged in and you can change its look right there:

  • An accent color (any color once you've used a Customizer Kit from Marquez's), a theme, a banner and a frame. The crown banner and the glowing frame are for players with a legendary or mythic achievement.
  • Paints from Marquez's Profile shelf (Holographic, TODD Terminal, Sunset, Void, Gold Rush, and the December ones). Try one on before you save.
  • Pins: show off up to three achievements, and pin a collectible.

In Discord, r!profile shows the same card. r!profile @player shows someone else's.

The share card

The Share button makes a big image of your profile in its own style, to copy or download.

Other players

On someone else's profile you can send them Polygons or offer a trade.

Hiding your profile

r!profile private takes yours off the site within a few minutes; r!profile private off brings it back.

Settings

The Settings tab works without logging in: switch off pop-ups, the peeking faces, holiday effects, shakes and glitches, or the pixel emojis. See Render Face.

Linked Roles

Servers can hand out roles from your real Rend-Conomy stats (Server Settings → Roles → Links → RendConomy): your net worth, achievement count, buildings, whether you founded a company, and days played, each from your best server. Link it once and the bot keeps it up to date.
