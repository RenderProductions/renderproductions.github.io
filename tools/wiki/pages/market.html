Trading hours

You can trade from 3 AM to 9 PM Eastern, every day. Overnight the market is after hours: prices still drift (a quarter as much), but nobody can buy or sell, and orders and brokers wait for the open. Now and then TODD halts trading on a company or the whole market after a big move.

Buying and selling

r!market shows every price; r!chart RRM draws the last day; r!compare puts them side by side. Buy with r!buy RRM 10 (or an amount of Polygons) and sell with r!sell RRM all. Big trades move the price against you a little. The live prices are also on the Rend-Conomy page.

Bastardly Glory is the wild one: it can soar, and it can crash.

Goals and news

Companies announce goals in the market news. Hit the goal and the stock jumps; miss it and it drops, and nobody knows the result ahead of time. Watch for breaking news, rumors that may or may not be true, and the odd story that shakes the whole market at once. Every company has good stretches and bad ones, and no stretch lasts forever. The Rend Times from Marquez's shop drops vague hints.

Dividends

Companies pay dividends per share as they do well. Every share you hold earns its part while you hold it; collect what's built up with r!dividends.

Limit and stop orders

Orders fill on their own while you're away (r!order):

  • Limit buy: buy a number of shares once the price falls to your limit. The money is set aside until it fills.
  • Limit sell: sell once the price rises to your target.
  • Stop: sell once the price falls to your stop, to cut a loss.

Up to 10 open at once; they expire after 7 days.

Short selling

Think a stock will fall? r!short BSTG 20 bets on it: you profit by however much it drops, and lose by however much it rises. Opening one holds half its value as margin, plus a 1% fee, and you can short up to 100 shares of a company. If your loss reaches 90% of the margin, the short is closed for you. r!cover closes one. If a company goes bankrupt, every short on it pays out in full.

TIPThe Bear Rumors and Fire Sale events make shorts free to open for an hour.
