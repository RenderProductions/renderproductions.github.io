Iso-Chess is chess as a roguelike, on a floating 3D board. March your army through three acts. The enemy shows its next moves, so you can dodge, block, or spend cards to turn the fight. Every piece you lose is gone for good.

A run

  • Three acts: the Rabble Fields, the Archers' Causeway and the Hollow Kingdom. Each is a map of battles, elites, camps and events, with a King at the end.
  • Telegraphs: the enemy shows what it's about to do. Read it and play around it.
  • Your army has names, and veterans earn traits. Lose a piece and it stays lost.
  • Goals change by fight: take the king, rout them, survive, or escort a piece across.
