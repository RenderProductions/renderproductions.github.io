Every house company on the market is also an employer. Get hired, work a shift every 15 minutes, answer the task it gives you, and climb from the bottom rank to the top. Your pay follows your company's share price, so a booming company pays better.

The jobs

Apply with r!apply <ticker>. Each company has its own kind of task and its own four ranks; the multiplier after each rank is how much more it pays than the first.

Shifts and tasks

r!work hands you a task: sort a mod queue, build a nacho order, put building steps in order, crunch numbers, spot a bug. Type the answer in the channel. You have about 20 seconds.

  • Right and fast: full pay, plus a tip of up to 50% for speed.
  • Wrong or too slow: you still get 40%.
  • Rush: after a right answer there's a 20% chance another task comes in (up to 5 in a row). Rush tasks pay only if they're right.
  • Coworkers: people working in the same channel within two minutes add 15% each (up to 3).
  • Pay: ⬡35 × the company's price compared to where it started × your rank. Bastardly Glory's swings are capped so its pay stays sane.

r!job shows your rank, accuracy, next review and next shift; r!job remind on pings you when a shift is ready.

Reviews and promotions

Every 10 shifts you get a review. Get 8 or more right and you're promoted; 4 or fewer and you're demoted. A great review at the top rank pays a bonus instead. You can quit 24 hours after being hired (r!quit), and you keep your rank if you come back.

LAYOFFSIf your company goes bankrupt, you're laid off with a few shifts' pay. A Hard Hat from Marquez keeps your job through one layoff.

Gigs and odd jobs

  • Gigs (r!gig): no job needed, every 10 minutes, pays 40% of a first-rank shift.
  • Odd jobs (r!oddjob): a math problem, an unscramble, a count or a reverse. Answer in the channel within 90 seconds; up to 10 a day.

Items that help

  • Coffee resets your work cooldown.
  • Overtime makes your next shift pay double.
  • Hard Hat keeps your job through one layoff.

Look out for the Labor Shortage and Hiring Spree world events, which raise pay for an hour.
