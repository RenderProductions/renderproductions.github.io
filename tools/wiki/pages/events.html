World events FROM THE BOT

Every few hours, one of these runs for an hour in every server. r!event shows the one running now.

December: Polygon Holidays

  • The advent calendar (r!advent): a door a day from December 1 to 25. Missed doors open late. Polygons, seeds, items and collectibles, and something special behind the last door.
  • Secret Santa (r!santa): sign up by December 14. On the 15th everyone who joined is matched up; wrap a gift of Polygons and an item, and it's delivered on Christmas Day.
  • The holiday shelf: Marquez stocks December-only collectibles and paints.
  • Snow Day: a December event with no work but double chat pay.

In October, pumpkins sell for 25% more.

Birthdays

Two parties a year, in every server. r!birthday shows the party and your server's cake. The birthday page has the full list.

  • RendConomy Big Birthday Blowout! November 9 to 15. RendConomy came to The Render Room on November 9, 2026, so the first one is in 2027.
  • Render Vision's Big Birthday! August 17 to 19, the developer's birthday.
  • Birthday Bash (November 9 and August 18): chat pay x1.5 all day, and a ⬡250 bonus on every daily.
  • The cake: every shift, gig, catch and harvest adds a piece to your server's cake. When it's full, everyone who helped is paid (⬡1,000 on RendConomy's birthday, ⬡500 on Render Vision's).
  • The Party shelf at Marquez's: Cake Slices (eat one for a little gift), a Party Hat (one per party), and on RendConomy's birthday a numbered Candle for the age it's turning.
  • Everywhere else: cake on the Slots reels, more in every progressive jackpot, Chuck's Party Fish in any water (it isn't part of the Fishdex), and TODD's birthday questions.
  • RendConomy's birthday only: the time capsule (r!birthday capsule <one line>, opened with your name at the next birthday) and r!yearbook, your RendConomy so far.
