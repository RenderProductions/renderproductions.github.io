World events FROM THE BOT

Every few hours, one of these runs for an hour in every server. r!event shows the one running now.

December: Polygon Holidays

  • The advent calendar (r!advent): a door a day from December 1 to 25. Missed doors open late. Polygons, seeds, items and collectibles, and something special behind the last door.
  • Secret Santa (r!santa): sign up by December 14. On the 15th everyone who joined is matched up; wrap a gift of Polygons and an item, and it's delivered on Christmas Day.
  • The holiday shelf: Marquez stocks December-only collectibles and paints.
  • Snow Day: a December event with no work but double chat pay.

In October, pumpkins sell for 25% more.
