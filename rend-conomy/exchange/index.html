How companies work

Prices and costs are marked ⬡; Polygons a company has are marked ⬢.

Anyone can start a company with r!found <ticker> <name> for ⬡ 2,500.00. A mod approves the name first, and if it's rejected you get your money back. Once approved, that money becomes the company's treasury.

The treasury pays a daily upkeep. It grows when the owner invests (r!invest), when players work there (every shift adds half the worker's pay), and from IPO share sales. Spend it to level up with r!upgrade.

At Firm level, the owner can go public with r!ipo (a ⬡ 2,000.00 listing fee). Then anyone can r!buy and r!sell its shares, except the owner. The first 400 shares sold fund the company. The owner can pay themselves up to 10% of the treasury a day with r!salary, but the stock dips when they do.

Miss an upkeep payment and the market panics. Keep missing them and the price slides to zero, and the company closes for good, taking every share with it. A private company closes after missing upkeep 3 days in a row.
