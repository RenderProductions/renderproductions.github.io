SETTINGS

CONTROLS

Move
← → or A D
Jump
Space, ↑ or W (jump off walls too)
Parry
↓, S, X, K or Shift
Phones
the buttons along the bottom

Everything he throws hurts unless you parry it. Parried attacks fly back and hit him: that’s the only way to hurt him. Three parries in a row without getting hit gives you one health back (up to 4).
