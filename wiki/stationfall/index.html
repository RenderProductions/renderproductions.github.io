Stationfall: your ship broke apart over an asteroid field. You and your crewmate Ade made it down with a shelter kit. Mine the rock, smelt it into bars, build out the station, keep the air on, take in survivors, and hold out when the scrapper drones come at night.

Controls

WASD to move, click to use what you're holding, E to interact, B to build, I for your inventory. The top row of the inventory is your hotbar (keys 1–6).

Staying alive

  • Watch your health, oxygen, food and stamina. Outside a sealed room the air runs out fast. A space suit stretches it.
  • Mine rock, ice, ore and crystal with the mining laser (the plasma cutter is faster), and smelt ore into bars at a furnace.
  • Build floors, walls, doors and airlocks to seal rooms, then power them (solar, batteries) and keep the air on (oxygen generators and tanks). Farms, medbays, workbenches, drills and turrets come after.
  • Crew: take in survivors and give them jobs.
  • Nights bring the scrapper drones. Turrets help. So does a rifle.
