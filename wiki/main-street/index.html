r!shop opens Main Street: Marquez's Shop, Chuck Alfrey's Bait & Tackle, the stores players run out of their Corner Shops, and the alley (at night). Each server has its own shelves, filled by Marquez's delivery truck. Prices move with what people buy, there's a deal every day and a sale every Friday, and the more you spend at Marquez's, the better he treats you.

Shopping

  • r!shop opens the street. Buttons take you to Marquez's, Chuck's, the player stores and your loyalty card.
  • At Marquez's, pick a shelf from the dropdown and an item from the second one to look closer: its price (and why), how many are left, and Buy 1, Buy 5 or Buy max.
  • By name: r!shop buy coffee 3 and r!shop sell coffee all. r!shop casino opens a shelf directly.
  • Selling back: Marquez pays ? of his list price, whatever the sale. Buying on sale and selling back never pays.

The delivery truck

Every server has its own stock now, so one busy server can't empty the shelves for everyone. The truck comes at ? Eastern and fills every shelf back up. A few minutes later it pulls up in a busy channel to say so (in the drop-in channel, if your server set one). The weekly collectible still changes once a week.

A Rush Order (r!use rush order coffee) still refills one item right away, for your server.

  • Black Friday, every Friday (Eastern): one shelf is featured at ? off and everything else is ? off. The featured shelf changes each week, in this order: ?.

Marquez's loyalty card

Everything you spend at Marquez's counter in a server counts toward your card there (r!shop card). He warms up as you go.

The back room: for Family, the rare items (? or fewer on the shelf) have as many again in the back, refilled with every delivery. When the shelf is empty, you buy from the back.

Wishlists

r!shop want coffee puts an item on your wishlist (up to ?); the same again takes it off, and r!shop want clear empties it. When a delivery brings something on it, you get a DM. Good Friends hear ? minutes before everyone else, and before the truck announces itself. Buying the item takes it off your list.

Chuck Alfrey's Bait & Tackle

Chuck has the Bamboo Rod and Lures (restocked by the same truck) and the better gear: rods, the Cooler and the Boat. Open it from Main Street or with r!fish shop, and buy with the buttons or r!fish buy carbon. See Fishing.

Chuck doesn't care what you spend; he cares what you've caught. He calls you a Greenhorn until you finish a page of the Fishdex, a Deckhand after one, a Skipper after three, and an Old Salt once it's all done.

Player stores

Every finished Corner Shop on the map is a store. Stock it from your inventory and set your own price. There's no cap: if you want to beat Marquez, you'll have to be smart about it.

  • r!shop stock coffee 3 at 150 puts 3 Coffee on your shelf at ⬡150 each. Add on C4 to pick which Corner Shop. Stocking an item you already sell adds to it and sets the new price.
  • r!shop unstock coffee (or unstock coffee 2) takes them back. r!shop store shows your shelves.
  • Slots per Corner Shop by level: ?.
  • Anything in your inventory can go on a shelf: Marquez's items, collectibles, crops, Goods. Fish stay in the fishing bag.
  • Buyers find you on Main Street, or go straight there with r!shop @you (and r!shop @you coffee 2 buys). They pay your price plus the mayor's sales tax, and you keep the whole price. Every sale sends you a receipt.
  • A Corner Shop under ?% condition, or with squatters, closes until it's fixed. If the building is sold, torn down or condemned, everything on its shelves comes back to your inventory.
