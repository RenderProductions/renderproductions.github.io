Render Productions

Story of Varandia is a tabletop world of Chaotic and Controlled magic, played with a d20. Raw, Chaotic magic gives the world its life. Train it into Controlled magic, carve it into runes, and carry it into the fight, so long as your Stamina holds. The rules are still being written.

The Tome

The rulebook, a chapter at a time: races, classes, stats and dice, magic and its eleven elements, the spellbook and the armory. The Bestiary lives in your GM's own book.

Characters

The builder walks you through it: roll your stats, pick a race and class, and fill in the sheet. Your sheet tracks Health and Stamina, your spells (cast straight from the sheet), your Satchel and Cardinal Items, and lets you roll from it. Your characters lists them all. Log in with Discord to keep them on any device.

Campaigns

A game master starts a campaign and shares its code; players join with their characters, and the GM sees everyone's sheet in one place. Add a Discord webhook and rolls post straight to your group's channel.
