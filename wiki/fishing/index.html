Get a rod and cast with r!fish. Pick a place (r!fish river) and a bait (r!fish river worm). You might catch nothing, snag your line, pull up junk, a lost wallet, a treasure chest or a message in a bottle, or a fish. Medium and bigger fish fight back: you reel them in with buttons. Every fish has its own weight, and heavier is worth more.

Where to fish

r!fish places shows which ones you can reach. The Lake is the default.

The Sewer is open to anyone, but every cast there adds 3 Heat (see the Underground). Ice fishing only opens in December.

Gear: Chuck Alfrey's Bait & Tackle

Chuck Alfrey has it all on Main Street: the Bamboo Rod and Lures, and everything better. r!fish shop, then r!fish buy carbon. Each rod tier also means fewer empty casts, bigger fish more often, and a line that holds more tension.

Bait

  • Plain bait costs ? a cast (free with a Shack's Bait Shed).
  • A Lure (from Chuck) lasts ? casts and brings bigger fish.
  • Worms are free: r!farm compost turns each piece of junk into ? worms (and Fertilizer). Small and medium fish love them. r!fish worm. Carrots from your farm work the same way.
  • A fish as bait brings out the next size up: r!fish perch uses your lightest Perch.

Reeling in

Small fish come straight in. Anything bigger is a fight: a Line in bar, a Tension bar, and two buttons.

  • Reel pulls it closer, but raises the tension.
  • Let out eases the tension, but gives a little line back.
  • Every round, the fish pulls (tension jumps), runs (it takes line) or rests.
  • Tension past what your rod can hold and the line snaps. Line in to 100% and it's yours. Let it get away, or take longer than 60 seconds, and it's gone.

Bigger fish fight harder, and legends hardest of all. Better rods hold more tension.

Every catch

A fish's worth scales with its weight. Prices also change every day (80–120%, the same for everyone), so the same fish sells for more on a good day.

Weights, giants and records

  • Every fish rolls a weight in its range, leaning to the middle.
  • ? of small, medium and large fish are giants: ? to ? times the normal weight, and they fight a bit harder.
  • Server records: the heaviest of each fish anyone has caught here. r!fish records.
  • Trophies: r!fish mount pike puts your heaviest Pike on your profile (it comes out of your bag, so you can't sell it).

The Fishdex

r!fish dex lists every fish by place, with a silhouette until you catch one and your heaviest after. Finishing a place's page pays a reward from Chuck Alfrey (in the table above). Catch every fish there is for Gotta Catch 'Em All!

The Saturday tournament

Every Saturday (Eastern time), enter with r!fish tournament join for ?. Your heaviest single fish that day counts. At midnight the Reserve adds ? to the entries, and the top 3 split the pot 60 / 30 / 10. r!fish tournament shows the standings.

Selling

  • r!fish sell sells your whole bag at today's prices. Junk sells for a little, or compost it.
  • Own a Warehouse and your fish stay fresh: they sell at the best price of the last 3, 5 or 7 days, by level.
  • Treasure chests pay out Polygons or a useful item; messages in bottles pay nothing, but they say things.

Perks and events

  • A Shack gives you free bait (Bait Shed). A Lighthouse makes plain bait fish like a Lure (Beacon), and opens the Pier without a Carbon Rod.
  • The Fish Frenzy event lets you cast every 90 seconds, and plain bait works like a Lure.
  • The mayor can fund a fishing luck public work: fewer empty casts and bigger fish for everyone.
