Buy a Fishing Rod from Marquez and cast with r!fish. Each cast costs a little bait unless you have a Lure on. You might catch nothing, snag your line, pull up junk, find a lost wallet, or land a fish. Sell your bag with r!sellfish.

Bait, lures and fish as bait

Plain bait is the cheapest way to fish. A Lure makes bigger fish more likely. Better still, a fish you've caught makes good bait for the next size up: r!fish perch. Carrots from your farm work like a small fish.

What you can catch

Use a fish as bait and catch a bigger one for There's Always a Bigger Fish, and land the White Whale for Moby Dick.

Perks

  • A Shack on the city map gives you free bait (Bait Shed).
  • A Lighthouse makes plain bait fish like a Lure (Beacon).
  • The Fish Frenzy event lets you cast every 90 seconds, and plain bait works like a Lure.
