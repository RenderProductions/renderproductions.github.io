Render's File Tycoon is a pipeline builder in your browser. Drag modules onto the board, wire them together, and turn downloaded files into Polygons. Prices follow the real Rend-Conomy market, and once you're logged in, what you earn can go into your RendConomy wallet.

Building a pipeline

  • Network modules (from WiFi up to fiber) give you bandwidth. Everything downstream shares it.
  • Downloaders pull one kind of file: Documents, Ebooks, Images, Audio, Archives, Video, Software or Databases. Bigger files are worth more and take longer.
  • Processors (scanner, antivirus, compressor, decoder, metadata tagger, encryptor) make files worth more, but use CPU and heat.
  • Upload & sell: an uploader and a market (basic, premium, auction, seeder, mirror) turn files into Polygons.
  • Storage and utilities (caches, buffers, splitters, sorters, schedulers) keep things flowing.

Drag from a port to wire two modules. Hover a module and press U to upgrade it, D to delete it. Middle-drag or hold Space to pan, scroll to zoom. The 3D view shows the same board in 3D.

Your PC

Everything runs on your PC, and its limits are real. Hardware Upgrades raise them: the CPU (how much processing you can run), RAM (buffers and parallel downloads), storage (disk space), the network card (bandwidth) and the cooler (heat; running hot throttles everything). The last tier of each is a joke price. Somebody will buy The Gregulator anyway.

The market

Each file type sells to one company, and pays more when that company's stock is up: BNW buys Documents and Databases, BLU Software and Archives, RRM Video and Audio, NACH Images and Ebooks. The sidebar's Market demand shows the live multipliers.

The RendConomy wallet

Log in with Discord (the button in the sidebar) and you can cash out: ⬡5 into your RendConomy wallet for every ⬡1,000 you've earned in the game, up to ⬡500 a day, paid from the Reserve. The bot remembers what it already paid, so the same earnings never pay twice. The rest keeps until tomorrow.

Six achievements unlock in the bot too: First Byte, Six Figures, The Gregulator, Fresh Install, Contractor, and a secret one.

The Terminal

The Terminal takes codes. Some are out there, if you know where to listen.
