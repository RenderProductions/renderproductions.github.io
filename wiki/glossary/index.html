Terms

Polygons (⬡)
The currency. Every server has its own Polygons.
The Reserve
The house's pot of Polygons: almost every payout comes from it and most spending goes back in. More.
Payout factor
The share of a payout that's actually paid. 100% normally; lower when the Reserve is under a quarter full.
Net worth
Everything you have: Polygons, savings, shares, buildings, minus what you owe.
Escrow
Polygons held safely by the bot while something is undecided, like a bid or a trade. You get them back if it falls through.
Shift
One round of work at your job: a task, then pay. Every 15 minutes.
Rush
A bonus task right after a shift, which only pays if you get it right.
Dividends
What shares earn while you hold them.
Short
A bet that a stock will fall.
Limit / stop order
An order to buy or sell automatically when a price is reached.
Grade
A crop's quality: Poor, Fine or Prime. It sets the range its price is rolled from.
Mutation
A rare crop that comes out Golden, Rainbow or Square and is worth many times more.
Tile
One square of a server's 8×8 city map, named like C4.
Condition
How well kept a building is, 0–100%. Rent scales with it.
Squatters
People living in a neglected building. No rent until they're gone.
Condemned
A building that fell to 0%. The bank takes it and auctions it.
Freight
Shipping your crops and Goods by train from your Station.
Goods
Crates a Factory makes. Sell them to Marquez or ship them.
Landmark
A big building the whole server funds together, with a perk for everyone.
VIP
Your Rend-Sino level, from everything you've ever bet. Comes with a daily free spin.
Progressive jackpot
A pot every bet in the server adds to. Three diamonds on Slots win it.
Heat
How much attention you've drawn from TODD, 0–100 (five stars). High Heat gets you raided.
Contraband
What the Connector sells: items that bend the rules.
Hot loot
Stolen valuables from heists. Fence or launder them before they get you caught.
World event
A change that runs for an hour in every server, like Happy Hour or a Storm.
