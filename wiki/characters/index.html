The Rend-Conomy has a narrator, and it has a cast. These are the ones you'll meet.

Marquez

Marquez runs the shop. He's reserved, kind and a little old-fashioned, and he calls you friend, pal, kiddo or champ. He stocks everything from coffee to fishing rods to seeds, buys your fish, crops and Goods, and sets out one collectible a week. He doesn't talk about the Connector.

Where: r!shop, r!sellfish, r!sellcrops.

TODD

TODD is a monitor with opinions. He's the house at the Rend-Sino, the lender of last resort (r!bailout), the law when the Underground gets out of hand, and the one who drops into chat with a question and a pot of Polygons. Win ten in a row at his casino and he'll have you escorted out. Ask him to rate something with r!rate; he'll do it badly. The server's first landmark is a statue of him.

The Connector

A red suit, a wide red hat, and in its shadow one red eye and one blue. Twitchy, paranoid, speaks in clipped whispers and never says names. He only comes out at night, and he never asks questions. See the Underground.
