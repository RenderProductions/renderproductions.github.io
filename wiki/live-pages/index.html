The Rend-Conomy pages update live from the bot, about every 30 seconds.

The market

The Rend-Conomy page shows every company's price, the market's status, the Reserve and the news. Each house company has its own page (RRM, NACH, BLU, BNW, BSTG) with a chart of the last week. Logged in, you can buy and sell right from the page.

The Exchange

The Exchange lists every player company: its ticker, founder, level, treasury and, once it's public, its share price. Each one has its own page with a trade box.

The city map

The city map shows each server's 8×8 grid: who built what, how it's holding up, the landmarks going up, and trains on the line. Click a tile for its details.

The bot and the patch notes

The bot's page has its live stats, every command, the shop and the whole emoji gallery. The patch notes list every update, newest first; the bot posts each one in Discord when it goes live.
