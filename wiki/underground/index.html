Every night from 10 PM to 4 AM Eastern, a figure in a red suit sets up somewhere on your server's city map. The Connector sells things Marquez won't, buys things nobody should, and never asks questions. Everything shady you do adds Heat, and Heat gets you raided.

At a glance
Open10 PM–4 AM Eastern
Commandr!ug
Heat0–100, five stars

Where he sets up

He squats in a condemned building if there is one; otherwise in the most run-down place on the map (its owner loses half that day's rent); otherwise on an empty lot. He won't set up within a tile of a Police Station. If stations cover the whole map, he takes an alley off the map instead: there's always a password that night, and every deal there brings 50% more Heat.

Password nights

About one night in three, you need a password to get in. From 8 PM, the Connector hijacks one of TODD's drop-ins to leave it, hidden in a spoiler. Get in with r!ug <password>.

Hot loot

Clean heists sometimes drop hot loot. Holding it adds Heat every day.

  • Fence it (r!ug fence): half its value when it's fresh, rising to 90% after three days.
  • Launder it through a Corner Shop you own (r!ug launder): its full value, a day later.

Smuggling

Once a night, put up between ⬡500 and ⬡10,000 (r!ug smuggle <amount>). Two hours later it comes back with 50% on top, 10% on top, or not at all (caught, with a lot of Heat). A Lighthouse lowers the chance of getting caught.

Snitching

For ⬡250, tip TODD off about another player (r!ug snitch @player). If they're at three stars or more, they're raided and you get a reward plus a share of their fine. If not, the Heat lands on you.

Heat and raids

Heat runs from 0 to 100, shown as stars, and cools by 10 a day. The more stars, the more likely a raid: TODD confiscates your contraband and loot and fines you a tenth of your Polygons (up to ⬡5,000). At 100 Heat you're locked out for two nights. r!ug heat shows yours.
