Someone plans a heist in a channel, others join with a role, and when the leader starts it everyone pays a stake. Then it plays out. A clean job splits the haul evenly; a failed one loses the stakes, and if TODD catches the crew, there's a fine on top.

At a glance

Per playerone every 6 hours
Per serverone an hour

Targets

r!heist vault, stash, reserve or train. Hauls come out of the Reserve, so robbing it really does hurt everyone.

Roles

Pick a role when you join. Each one adds to the crew's odds:

Robbing a train

When a Station owner ships freight on the city map, the train spends half an hour on open track. A crew can rob it in that window and take up to half of what it carries. The owner still gets the rest when it arrives. You can't rob your own train.

Hot loot

A clean heist sometimes drops hot loot for each crew member: a ring, a painting, a gem or a crate. It's worth a lot, but holding it draws Heat. Fence it or launder it through the Underground.

TIPA Lockpick Set adds 15% to your next heist, and the Police Strike event adds 15% for everyone for an hour.
