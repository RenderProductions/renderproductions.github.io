Who's whoYour first day

Marquez's shelves restock every hour, and his stock is shared by every server, so popular items can sell out. One collectible is on sale each week, one per customer. Buy with r!purchase <item> [amount] and use with r!use <item> [amount]; items marked "works on its own" do their thing just by being in your inventory.

  • Using lots at once: r!use mystery 10 or r!use mystery all opens up to ? in one message. Coffee, the Customizer Kit, the Megaphone, paints and the Rush Order go one at a time.
  • Rush Order: r!use rush order coffee has Marquez restock that one item to full, for everyone. Not for collectibles, and you keep the Rush Order if the item is already full.
  • Selling back: r!sellitem <item> [amount|all]. Marquez pays ? of his current price and puts it back on his shelf. He doesn't buy collectibles (trade those with r!trade), and fish and crops have their own buyers.
  • Throwing away: r!discard <item> [amount|all], for nothing.
