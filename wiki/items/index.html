Each server has its own stock, filled by Marquez's delivery truck three times a day, and prices move with demand (see Main Street). The prices below are list prices. One collectible is on sale each week, one per customer. Buy with r!shop buy <item> [amount] and use with r!use <item> [amount]; items marked "works on its own" do their thing just by being in your inventory.

  • Using lots at once: r!use mystery 10 or r!use mystery all opens up to ? in one message. Coffee, the Customizer Kit, the Megaphone, paints and the Rush Order go one at a time.
  • Mystery: ? of the time it holds ?, ? an Overtime pass, and otherwise ⬡?–?. On average that's about what it costs.
  • Rush Order: r!use rush order coffee has Marquez restock that one item to full, for everyone in your server. Not for collectibles, and you keep the Rush Order if the item is already full.
  • Selling back: r!shop sell <item> [amount|all]. Marquez pays ? of his list price and puts it back on his shelf. He doesn't buy collectibles (trade those with r!trade, donate them to the museum, or sell them to Mr. Glaz), and fish and crops have their own buyers.
  • Throwing away: r!discard <item> [amount|all], for nothing.

Every item FROM THE BOT

Collectibles: collector value and the museum

Every collectible has a collector value, worked out once a day from how many exist across every server: rarer than the average collectible, it's worth more (up to 5× Marquez's price); more common, less (down to half). It counts toward your net worth. r!collection shows yours, and the museum page has today's prices.

  • The museum (r!museum): donate a collectible with r!museum donate <collectible>. It's gone for good, you're paid 75% of its value, and the first of each kind puts your name on the plaque. Each kind on display gives the server +0.5% chat and shift pay.
  • The wings: Founders' Wing (every weekly special): +5% property rent. Winter Wing (the holiday collectibles): advent doors pay double Polygons. Birthday Wing (the Party Hat and every Candle so far): the birthday cake needs 20% fewer pieces. The Full Collection: +2% on every achievement reward.
  • Mr. Glaz buys collectibles at night for 125% of their value (r!ug sell <collectible>): one a night, only ones you've had for a week, with Heat. What he buys is gone for good.
  • Collectibles are never loan collateral.
