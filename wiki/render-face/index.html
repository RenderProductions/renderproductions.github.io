Profiles and the customizer

Render Face is a big robot head with a screen for a face. Its eyes follow your pointer and it blinks now and then. It has opinions about what you do with that pointer.

The face

  • It frowns when your pointer leaves the window.
  • It gets annoyed if you keep clicking it, and dizzy if you circle it.
  • Little alert windows pop up from time to time. Press Escape to close the newest one.

Around the site

Its little ones peek in from the edges of other pages and run when you get close. Catch one before it escapes and it's deleted, into ones and zeros. The face keeps count.

Pop-ups show up across the site too, in one of three looks (pixel, Win95 or CRT). Every one of them has a Make it stop link.

Turning it off

The Settings tab on your profile page (no login needed) switches off pop-ups, their sounds, the peeking faces, the tab titles that beg you to come back, the holiday lights and snow, shakes and glitches, and the pixel emojis. It also picks the pop-up style.
