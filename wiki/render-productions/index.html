Render Productions is an indie dev hub for The Render Room on Discord, run by friends who may or may not know what they're doing. It's owned by Chace "Render Vision" Page. Everything here, the bot, the games and the site, is made for that community.

The Render Room

The Render Room is where it all happens: the bot lives there, updates are announced there, and that's where to find the team. Join it from the Discord page.

Updates and patch notes

Updates come often. Each one gets a name and a patch note; Quick Adds are small ones and Hotfixes fix something. The patch notes list them all, and the bot posts each one in Discord when it goes live. Player ideas make it in all the time.

Found a bug?

Tell a moderator in The Render Room. Please don't use it to get ahead: the terms ask you to report bugs instead of exploiting them.

The Featured page

The wiki's home page features one page a week, and the players pick it: r!wiki vote <page> in Discord (once a week, across every server). The most votes this week wins next week. r!wiki shows the race.

The fine print

The terms of service and the privacy policy cover the bot and the site.
