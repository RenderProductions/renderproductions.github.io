The Play hub is Rend-Conomy in your browser. Log in with Discord, pick a server you play in, and everything you do runs through the bot as you, exactly like a command in chat.

The tabs

  • Desk: your balance, the daily, dividends, savings, loans, your history and your showcase.
  • Main Street: Marquez's, Chuck's and the player stores, and your inventory.
  • Farm: plant, water, harvest, sell, fertilize and compost, buy plots, and run your farmhand.
  • City: your buildings on the map, building and upgrading, freight, Goods and the landmark.
  • Odd Jobs, Trades and your Inbox.

The server picker at the top decides where your actions go. A live price strip runs along the top.

The bell

Logged in, a bell sits in the top bar of every page. It lights up when something lands in your inbox (receipts, trade offers, reports, ready crops) and opens it.

Logging in

Log in with Discord only tells the site your Discord name and picture. It never sees your password or your messages. See the privacy policy for exactly what happens.
