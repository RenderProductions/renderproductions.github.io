Picking a path

On this page
The workerThe farmerThe anglerThe traderThe landlordThe gamblerThe crook

There's no class to pick and you can do everything at once. But most players lean one way. Here's what each path feels like, roughly from the safest to the wildest.

The worker

Shifts every 15 minutes, gigs every 10, odd jobs and promotions. Steady, needs you to be around, and the pay follows your company's share price. Start: r!apply. Work →

The farmer

Buy seeds, plant, water, harvest, sell. Crops grow in real time from 30 minutes to a day, come out Poor, Fine or Prime, and sometimes mutate into something worth five or twelve times as much. Hire a farmhand and it runs while you're away. Start: seeds from r!shop garden, then r!farm. Farming →

The angler

A Fishing Rod and a few Polygons of bait per cast. Small fish make good bait for bigger ones, and somewhere out there is the White Whale. Start: a Rod from r!shop tackle, then r!fish. Fishing →

The trader

Buy and sell shares in the five house companies (and players' companies), collect dividends, set limit and stop orders, hire a broker, or short a stock you think will fall. Start: r!market. The Market → · Player companies →

The landlord

Claim tiles on your server's city map and build: Shacks, shops, Stations, Factories, Mansions. Rent comes in daily; upkeep, tax, neighbors and rivals' buyout bids keep it interesting. Start: ⬡1,000 of land plus a building. The City →

The gambler

The Rend-Sino: slots, blackjack, roulette, the Rocket and more, a progressive jackpot, and VIP levels with a free spin a day. Every game's odds are published, and win ten in a row and TODD throws you out. The Rend-Sino →

The crook

Heists with a crew, and at night, the Connector's black market: contraband, hot loot, smuggling and snitching. Every shady move adds Heat, and Heat gets you raided. Heists → · The Underground →
