The daily allowance

r!daily pays ⬡250 once per day; the day turns over at midnight Eastern. Claim it every day to build a streak.

Chat pay and drop-ins

Talking in the server pays ⬡5 to ⬡15 a message, once a minute, up to ⬡300 a day. Very short messages don't count. Now and then TODD drops in with a question and a pot of Polygons for whoever answers first. A Mansion party, the Stadium landmark and the Gossip Hour event all raise chat pay.

Savings

Lock Polygons away and get them back with interest: r!savings deposit 1000 7. Up to 3 accounts and ⬡50,000 in all, from ⬡100 each. A Bank Branch on the city map adds 25% per level to your interest.

Loans

  • From TODD: r!bailout when you're nearly broke: ⬡500 now, ⬡600 owed. No gambling until it's paid (r!repay).
  • From another player: r!loan @player 1000 5 asks for ⬡1,000 at 5% a day (up to 25%); they accept or decline. Loans are due in a week. Overdue, and half of what you earn from work, dailies and dividends goes to the lender until it's paid. r!loans lists yours; a lender can r!forgive.

Gifts and trades

  • Gifts: r!gift @player 500 sends Polygons with a 5% fee, up to ⬡5,000 a day.
  • Trades: offer Polygons and/or an item for someone else's (r!trade @player 500 for rod). What you offer is held safely until they answer; offers expire in a day (you get it back), and you can have three open.

Your inbox

r!inbox collects what happened while you were away: receipts, price alerts, bids on your buildings, ready crops, trade offers. On the site, the bell in the top bar shows new ones. r!receipts on also sends receipts by DM.

Profile and leaderboards

A Title item sets the line under your name (r!settitle); r!settitle clear removes it. r!profile shows your net worth, achievements and pinned showcase (r!showcase). Customize its color, theme, banner and frame, or edit it on your profile page on the site. r!leaderboard ranks the server by net worth, Polygons or shifts.

Playing from the site

Log in with Discord on the Play hub to work, farm, build, trade and read your inbox from a browser, and open the Trading Floor Activity right inside Discord.
