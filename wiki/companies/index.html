Any player can found a company. It has a treasury, levels up as you invest, and at level 3 it can go public: other players can buy its shares, and it gets its own page on the exchange.

Listing fee⬡2,000
Shares1,000 (400 for sale)

Founding one

r!found TICK Your Company Name sends it to the server's moderators for approval; if the name is rejected you get your money back. Once approved, that money becomes the company's treasury. See it with r!company.

The treasury grows when you invest (r!invest), when players work there (every shift adds half the worker's pay, and anyone can r!apply TICK), and from share sales after it goes public.

Levels

Upgrades are paid from the treasury (r!upgrade). Every level costs upkeep each day, and raises what the company can pay.

Going public

At level 3 (Firm), r!ipo lists the company. Anyone but the owner can then buy and sell its shares, and the first 400 shares sold fund the company. Its price moves with trading, and its treasury earns from revenue: farmers' market sales, a Corner Shop's Storefront on the city map, and more. Once it's public, your employees' pay follows the share price like a house company's: double the IPO price and shifts pay double, from ?× up to ?× the normal pay. You can pay yourself a salary of up to 10% of the treasury a day (r!salary), but the stock dips when you do.

Upkeep and closing

The treasury pays upkeep every day. Miss a payment and the market panics; keep missing them and the price slides to zero and the company closes for good, taking every share with it. A private company closes after missing upkeep three days in a row.

Closing it yourself

r!closecompany shows what you'd get; r!closecompany confirm does it. A public company first buys out every shareholder at the share price, from the treasury and then your wallet if the treasury runs short. You get what's left of the treasury minus a ? closing fee. The levels you paid for are gone, employees lose their jobs, and you can't found another company for ? days.

Selling it

r!sellcompany @player <price> offers the whole company (treasury, level and staff) to another player in your server. They have a day to answer with r!sellcompany accept or decline; r!sellcompany cancel takes it back. The buyer can't already run a company, and has to sell any shares they hold in it first. No sale while a takeover bid is open.

NPC workers

A company can hire staff who earn for its treasury every day, even when nobody's playing. r!company staff lists them; r!company hire hires one for ? from the treasury.

  • How many: ? at Startup, ? at Small Business, ? at Firm, ? at Corporation and up.
  • Wages: paid from the treasury every day. The going rate is ?; you can set anything from ? to ? with r!company wage <name|all> <amount>.
  • What they earn: about ? a day each, times the company's level bonus, and more when they're happy (half as much at their unhappiest, half again at their happiest).
  • Moods: pay at least the going rate and moods hold or rise (well above it, they rise fast). Underpay them, or let the treasury run dry, and moods fall. Under ? they may go on strike for a day (no work, no pay). At 0 they quit, and they tell you why.
  • r!company fire <name> lets one go.

Takeovers and mergers

If someone is crashing your company to take it over cheap, go to City Hall: the mayor can block the bid, freeze trading, and fine or investigate whoever is behind it.

  • Hostile takeover: a player who holds at least half of the shares for sale can bid for your company with r!takeover, putting up a premium of a quarter of its worth. You have a day to answer; no answer and it goes through.
  • Poison pill: defend for a tenth of the company's worth (r!takeover defend). The bidder gets their premium back minus a 20% break fee, which goes to you, and your company is shielded from takeovers for 7 days.
  • Merger: two owners can agree to merge with r!merge.
