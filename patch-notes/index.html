Room to Grow

Real estate comes to the Rend-Conomy. Every server gets one map of 15 lots, and there's no limit on how many you can own. Buy the whole block if you can afford it. Keeping it is another story. See the live map.

THE BLOCK

  • Six Shacks (⬡2,500) with a Bait Shed: free bait when you fish.
  • Four Apartments (⬡8,000) with an Extra Unit: +25% rent while they're kept above 75%.
  • Three Corner Shops (⬡20,000) with a Storefront: pick a player company to earn 10% of the rent.
  • One Lighthouse (⬡45,000) with a Beacon: rarer fish on plain bait, and storms do half the damage to everything you own.
  • One Mansion (⬡120,000) with a Ballroom: throw a party once a day and chat pay doubles for everyone in the server for an hour.
  • Rent is paid every day. Places wear down about 2% a day, more in storms, and rent drops with them. Keep them up with r!property repair.

KEEPING IT IS THE HARD PART

  • Property tax climbs with every lot: nothing for one or two, then 0.5%, 1% and 1.5% of everything's value a day.
  • Full sets: own every lot of one kind for +50% rent on all of them. The whole server hears about it.
  • Buyout bids: anyone can offer 25% over a place's value. The owner has 24 hours to refuse, which costs them 15% of the bid and shields the place for a week. No answer, and it's sold.
  • Squatters move into places left under 40%. No rent until you evict them or fix the place up.
  • Condemned: a place that falls apart is taken by the bank and auctioned to the server for 24 hours.

ALSO NEW

  • NEWThe live map on the site shows every lot, its owner and its shape, updating as it happens. Play from the new Property tab on the Play hub.
  • NEWAchievements: First Home, Landlord, Lord of the Manor, Fixer-Upper, Full House, Monopoly, Baby!, and a secret one.
  • CHANGEDProperty counts toward your net worth.

Plants? In THIS Economy??!

RendConomy has farming. Every player gets a field of their own in each server: plant it, water it, keep the crows off it and sell what it grows to Marquez.

YOUR FARM

  • /farm shows your field as a grid of plots, what each one is doing, and your harvest basket.
  • Plots: you start with 3. Buy up to 9 with /buyplot; each one costs more than the last.
  • Seeds are on Marquez's new Garden shelf. /plant carrot fills every empty plot, or name a plot.
  • /water once while a crop sprouts and once while it grows. Thirsty crops grow at half speed, and the slow ones wilt if they're left dry too long.
  • /harvest picks everything that's ready, and /farm sell sells the basket to Marquez.
  • DM reminders: /farm remind on and the bot lets you know when crops are ready.

THE CROPS

  • Carrots: 30 minutes. They also make good fishing bait: /fish carrot.
  • Corn: 2 hours.
  • Tomatoes: 4 hours, then they grow back twice for 3 harvests.
  • Pumpkins: 12 hours, and a big payout.
  • Polygon Fruit: a full day, and Marquez only gets a couple of seeds an hour. Worth it.

MUTATIONS

  • Golden crops sell for 5 times as much.
  • Rainbow crops are rarer, and worth 12 times as much.
  • Once in a while a pumpkin comes out square. Nobody knows how. Marquez would like to know how.

TROUBLE

  • Crows sometimes come for a growing crop. A Scarecrow chases them off your whole farm.
  • Rain is a new world event: it waters every crop while it falls.
  • Storms are one too, and can flatten crops. A Greenhouse keeps them safe.

UPGRADES

  • Sprinkler: waters everything for you, forever.
  • Fertilizer: one crop grows 50% faster. /fertilize.
  • /compost turns fishing junk into Fertilizer. That Soggy Boot finally has a purpose.

ACHIEVEMENTS

  • NEW21 farming achievements in their own section, from Green Thumb to Taste the Rainbow, plus a secret one for the unluckiest farmers.

ALSO

  • CHANGED/bal and /portfolio are one view now: cash, savings, shares and net worth together. /pf and /wallet still work.
  • FIXED/fish bag is back: your catch, what it's worth, and the bag count. The inventory sums it up as one Fishing bag line.
  • FIXEDBot replies keep their blank lines again, so longer replies are easier to read.
