The Penny Pinchers Update

REND-CONOMY BOT

  • NEWTODD Loans. Broke? If your net worth is under 100, r!bailout borrows 500 Polygons from TODD, and you owe him 600. Pay him back with r!repay <amount or all>. No r!slots until you do. He'll remember. (Mostly.)

  • CHANGEDBNW shifts got harder: the numbers now need adding, subtracting, or multiplying.

The Payday Update

REND-CONOMY BOT

  • NEWJobs. Work for one of the four companies with r!apply, clock in with r!work every 45 minutes, and climb from Intern to Executive. Pay follows your company's stock price. Suggested by NachoBellGrande1065.
  • NEWShop. r!shop and r!purchase: coffee, overtime passes, mystery boxes, resumes, and custom titles. Stock is shared by every server and restocks hourly.
  • NEWDividends. Every share now pays out daily, and smaller companies pay more (BNW 2.5%, NACH 1.5%, BLU 1%, RRM 0.5%). Collect with r!dividends. Failed goals freeze a company's dividend for 24 hours; successful ones pay a bonus.
  • CHANGEDThe market is now open every day, 3 AM to 9 PM Eastern. No more closed Sundays.
  • CHANGEDr!chart charts now show times along the bottom.
