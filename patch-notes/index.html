The Fishy Deep

WHERE TO FISH

  • Time and weather matter. Catfish and eels bite at night, rain stirs things up, and storms bring the legends out.

OLD BARNABY'S BAIT & TACKLE

Under New Management

Every server elects a mayor now, with real power over its economy. Companies can hire staff. Lenders can finally collect, and Warehouses can order seeds wholesale. Under New Management and collections were NachoBellGrande1065's ideas, Wholesale was KorbedOut's, and you all asked for workers.

ELECT A MAYOR

  • Weekly elections, in every server. Run Monday to Thursday with r!mayor run [slogan]; vote Friday to Sunday with r!vote @player. Results come in Sunday at 9 PM Eastern. Anyone can vote. To run, you need to have played on 3 of the last 7 days, with an account at least a week old, and you can serve 2 terms in a row before sitting one out.
  • The mayor's powers (r!mayor help): block a takeover or merger, freeze a company's trading for a day, investigate a player's trades, fine a company or a player, and bail out a company being crashed. Set taxes on Marquez's shop (up to 15%), property (up to 3%) and casino winnings (up to 15%). Design public works (a rent boost, chat pay, shift pay, crop prices, or Happy Hour). Issue decrees, and appoint a Treasurer and a Police Chief.
  • The limits: the mayor can never pay themselves from the city treasury, can't use business powers where they have a stake, and everything goes on the public record (r!mayor log). Don't like your mayor? r!impeach opens a 24-hour vote.
  • NEWA City Hall achievement section: I Voted, Elected!, Four More Years, Landslide, Trustbuster, Public Works, and a secret one.

NPC WORKERS

  • Hire staff for your company: r!company hire. They earn for your treasury every day, even when nobody's playing. Up to 2 at Startup and 8 at the top.
  • Treat them right. Each worker has a name and a mood. Pay the going rate or better and they work harder; underpay them and they strike, then quit, and they'll tell you why. r!company staff, r!company wage, r!company fire.

COLLECT CALL

  • r!collect @borrower: a few days after an overdue loan's due date, the lender can make the borrower pay from what they own: their wallet, then savings, shares, and shop items. Never their buildings, company or collectibles. The lender gets exactly what they're owed, and a collection fee goes to the city. The mayor sets the fee (5–20%, 10% to start) and the grace period (1–5 days, 3 to start).
  • Borrowers get a DM when a loan goes overdue, and both sides get one when it can be collected. New achievement: Repo Man.

WHOLESALE SEEDS

  • Own a Warehouse? Order seeds from the supplier: r!property wholesale polyfruit 3. It never touches Marquez's shelf, so there's no racing anyone. Your own daily allowance of every seed (10, 25 or 50 by Warehouse level; Polygon Fruit 3, 6 or 10), at 10% over Marquez's price.

  • CHANGEDThe wiki looked like an office handbook. It's back in the site's own colors: navy and gold, terminal type, bracketed panels and the CRT scanlines, just turned down a little for long reading.

Story of Varandia (Beta)

Varandia is a tabletop game now, and its rulebook lives on the site as an old, creased book. Read the rules, make a character and roll straight from the sheet. The rules are still being written, so expect pages to change.

THE BOOK

  • NEWStory of Varandia: a two-page spread with ribbon bookmarks, a contents page and chapters on Races, Stats & Dice, and Magic with the eleven elements.
  • NEWRaces: Humans, Emberkin, Glacians, Floralians and the Stoneborn, with their stat modifiers, heights and a few words on each.
  • NEWRolling: d20 + half the stat. Defense, Health, Stamina, Initiative and Movement are all worked out from your stats.
  • NEWThe Spellbook: over 300 spells from the D&D System Reference Document, sorted into Varandia's two kinds of magic: Controlled (the eleven elements, with Varandia's own Evil spells, which all cost blood) and Chaotic (raw magic with no element). Each comes with its Stamina cost and the roll you need.

YOUR CHARACTERS

  • NEWMake a character: roll a d6 for every stat (or type in your real dice), spend 20 points, pick a race.
  • NEWCharacter sheets: four spreads behind tabs on the page edge. The Fight: tap a stat to roll it, type damage or healing, track Stamina, conditions, Exhaustion (which takes from every roll) and Taint. Spells: your spells grouped by element, read any of them and cast. Satchel: gear, Cardinal Items and proficiencies. Story: background, looks, personality, ideals, bonds, flaws, your story, notes and a body count. Characters are kept in your browser, and on your Discord account once you log in.
  • NEWThe Armory: weapons, armor, gear and common-to-rare magic items from the D&D System Reference Document, healing potions and Varandia's own Stamina Tonics, the thirteen Relics of Ward with Varandia rules, and the ten Elemental Masks. Put a weapon in your Satchel and it gets an attack button that rolls to hit and damage; wear armor and a shield for more Defense; drink a potion or tonic and it rolls for you. Write +1 Longsword and the bonus is added.
  • NEWGunpowder arms: from the Pirate Era through the Late Renaissance: the Flintlock Rifle, Blunderbuss, Pepperbox and Duck-foot Pistols, Hand Mortar, Cutlass, Boarding Axe and Pike, Bayonet and Belaying Pin, plus Shot & Powder, Powder Bombs and a Gunsmith's Kit. Firearms can misfire on a natural 1. Items from D&D's own world (elven, dwarven, Valhalla) are gone.
  • NEWClasses: the twelve D&D classes. Each adds Health every level, leans on certain stats and makes the spells on its list easier to cast.
  • NEWFlesh and bone: the character builder has a second page for class, a background you write yourself (with what they're good at and what they carry), and looks, personality, ideals, bonds, flaws and a backstory.
  • NEWProficiency bonus: +2 at level 1, up to +6, added to rolls for anything you're proficient in. Tap "Add proficiency" on your sheet before the roll.
  • NEWChaos Surges: Chaotic spells start harder, get easier every two levels, and on a natural 1–3 they surge. The sheet rolls the surge for you.
  • NEWLevelling up: the GM says when. Each level gives you a point to spend, and every stat with a training mark grows too. Rolling a natural 20 marks a stat; the GM can mark one after a hard roll. Up to level 20.
  • NEWCasting: learn spells from the Spellbook and cast them from your sheet. It rolls for you, takes the Stamina (and the blood), and takes your health if it fizzles.

CAMPAIGNS

  • NEWSaved to your account: log in with Discord on Your characters and your characters follow you to any device.
  • NEWCampaigns: a GM starts a campaign and gets a six-letter join code. Players join with the code and pick a character. The GM sees the whole party at once (Health, Stamina, Defense, conditions and the last roll) and can open and edit any character in the campaign.
  • NEWRolls to Discord: the GM pastes a Discord webhook link, and players can switch on "post rolls to Discord" on their sheets. Every roll then shows up in that channel under the character's name: the total in big numbers, spells in their element's colour, gold for a natural 20, Chaos Surges and fizzles spelled out, and the character's Health and Stamina underneath.
  • NEWGM screen: on the campaign page, only the GM sees a dice tray. Type who's rolling and any dice (d20+4, 2d8+3), or tap d20 to d4. Keep a roll secret, or send it to the campaign's Discord channel under that name, so the Bandit Captain's attack shows up as the Bandit Captain. Save the rolls you use a lot so they're a tap away, and press Track to keep an NPC's Health and Stamina, which then show under their rolls in Discord.
  • NEWNPCs: GMs press + New NPC on their campaign to make a full sheet for a notable figure: any level, any stats, class optional, with spells, weapons and a portrait. NPCs start hidden from players; show one and players see who they are (not their numbers) under "Met along the way". They roll for initiative on their own, and their rolls go to Discord under their name.
  • NEWInitiative: the GM presses Roll initiative on the campaign page. Players' Initiative rolls from their sheets slot in by themselves, tracked NPCs roll with their own bonus, and anyone else can be added by name. Next turn moves down the order and counts rounds. Players see the order on their campaign page and are told when it's their turn, and with "to Discord" on, each turn is posted to the channel.
  • NEWPortraits: tap the frame at the top of your sheet to give your character a picture. It shows on your character list and the GM's party view, and your rolls in Discord use it as the character's picture.

No Questions Asked

Somebody moved into the condemned lot. Red suit. Wide red hat. One red eye, one blue. He doesn't give his name, so everyone calls him the Connector. Marquez won't talk about him.

THE CONNECTOR

  • Every night from 10 PM to 4 AM Eastern he squats in a condemned lot on your server's map. Nothing condemned? He picks the most run-down place, and its owner loses half that day's rent. r!underground (or r!ug).
  • Some nights there's a password. He leaves it by taking over one of TODD's drop-ins. Keep your eyes open after 8 PM.

WHAT HE DEALS IN

  • Contraband, four of seven each night: Loaded Dice, a Fake ID, a Lockpick Set, an Insider Tip, the Rumor Mill, a Smoke Bomb and a Squatter Crew.
  • Hot loot. Clean heists can turn up stolen goods. Marquez won't touch them. The Connector will: half price while they're hot, more if you wait, if you're not raided first.
  • Laundering through a Corner Shop you own: the full value, a day later, with less Heat.
  • Smuggling runs across the lake, once a night. Two hours later you're up 50%, up 10%, or out of luck. Owning the Lighthouse keeps the patrol looking the other way.
  • Snitching. Tip TODD off about someone running hot and he pays you. Get it wrong and he looks at you instead.

HEAT

  • Everything shady adds Heat, shown as five stars. It cools off a little each day, unless you're sitting on hot loot.
  • TODD raids. The hotter you are, the likelier he kicks the door in: contraband and loot confiscated, and a fine. Hit five stars and the Connector won't deal with you for two nights.
  • NEWAchievements: Password, Please · Busted! · Fence Sitter · Most Wanted · Smuggler's Run · Clean Hands · and a secret one.
