LATESTNovember 10, 2026#

Save the Rend-Conomy

THE POLYGON RESERVE

  • NEWThe house is not infinite anymore. Every Polygon the bot pays out comes from one shared Polygon Reserve, and every Polygon you pay it goes back in. Every server shares the same one. Check it with r!reserve.
  • NEWWhat drains it: work, daily, dividends, slots wins, mystery box prizes, TODD bailouts, and selling stock back to the house. What fills it: buying stock, the shop, slots losses, repaying TODD, and company upkeep and fees.
  • NEWKeep it alive. Warnings go out in the market feed at 50%, 25% and 10%. Under 25%, every payout shrinks, down to as little as 10%. If it ever hits zero, the market crashes. The crash's recovery slowly refills it.
  • CHANGEDThe bot's own portfolio shows the real reserve now. The house always wins. Until it runs out.

MARKET

  • NEWMarket booms. The opposite of a crash: for events, the whole board, or just a few companies, can get a boost that lifts prices for a few hours, with its own 🚀 MARKET BOOM news. It even works during a crash or its recovery.
  • CHANGEDThe market now lives inside Render's Vision. Prices move every 5 minutes, right on time, and the bot reacts to the market the moment it changes. The hidden market state started fresh with the move, so nobody knows what's coming.
  • CHANGEDr!market shows the Polygon Reserve and any boom, and the bot's status keeps an eye on both.

SITE

  • NEWA Polygon Reserve meter and a MARKET BOOM banner on the market page, and boom news on every company page.

A New Vision

PLAYER COMPANIES

  • NEWStart your own company with r!found <ticker> <name> for ⬡ 2,500. A mod approves the name first, and you get your money back if it's turned down. Idea by nachobellgrande1065.
  • NEWGrow it from Startup to Small Business, Firm, Corporation, and Conglomerate with r!invest and r!upgrade. Anyone can r!apply to work there, and every shift earns the company money.
  • NEWGo public. At Firm level, r!ipo lists your company so anyone can buy and sell its shares, except you. Pay yourself with r!salary, but investors notice.
  • NEWKeep it alive. Companies pay upkeep every day. Miss it and the stock crashes. When a company's share price hits zero, it's gone for good, along with every share of it.
  • NEWr!company, r!exchange, and the new Exchange page show every player company.

LOANS

  • NEWBorrow from other players with r!loan @player <amount> <interest>, like r!loan @KorbedOut 1000 4 for 4% a day (25% max). The lender accepts or declines with a button. Idea by KorbedOut.
  • NEWInterest grows every day for a week. Pay early with r!payback. Miss the deadline and half of everything you earn (work, daily, dividends) goes to your lender until it's paid. See it all with r!loans, or let someone off the hook with r!forgive.

BOT

  • NEWReceipts in your DMs for trades, shop purchases, loans, and company investments and salaries, so you can track where your Polygons went. Don't want them? r!receipts off.
  • NEWButtons. Answer Render Room, Bastardly Glory and BLU work tasks with a tap (typing still works), spin the slots again, buy straight from r!shop, and confirm before you r!quit or r!ipo.
  • NEWInventory. Shop items go into your inventory now, and you use them when you want with r!use <item>. See what you've got with r!inventory. Mystery boxes stay sealed until you open them.
  • CHANGEDShop prices: Coffee ⬡ 100, Overtime ⬡ 250, Resume ⬡ 350, Title ⬡ 5,000. Mystery boxes cost ⬡ 75 to ⬡ 100, and the price changes every restock.
  • CHANGEDr!daily now resets at midnight Eastern instead of 24 hours after you claim it.
  • CHANGEDr!portfolio is now r!balance (r!bal and r!portfolio still work). Balances keep their cents everywhere.
  • NEWPolygons have symbols. ⬢ is what you have (your Polygons, what's left after a trade, net worth). ⬡ is what's moving (prices, costs, payouts, debts).
  • NEWThe bot's status shows live prices, market hours, and the odd TODD thought, and it goes idle while the market is closed.

The Crash

REND-CONOMY

  • NEWThe market is crashing. Every company is heading down. Nobody knows how far, or who makes it out. Panic sell-offs, fake bounces, and failed goals are all part of the ride. It will end. Eventually.
  • NEWPrices can go below zero. Under 3.00, a stock moves in Polygons instead of percent, so it can hit zero and keep falling. A company that hits zero is BANKRUPT, and every share of it is wiped from every portfolio. Gone. Sell before it's too late.
  • NEWBankruptcy. A bankrupt company can't set goals, pays no dividend, and can't be traded. It has two ways out: beg TODD for a bailout, or climb back above 1.00 on its own and hold it for an hour.
  • NEWTODD bailouts for companies. A bankrupt company files an appeal, and TODD rules on it an hour or two later. Every denial sinks it deeper, but the odds get better with each try (and worse while the crash is still going). Approved, trading restarts at a few Polygons and TODD keeps the dividends for a day.
  • NEWCircuit breakers. If the whole board drops too far in one hour, trading halts for 15 minutes and prices freeze.
  • NEW"On the brink" warnings when a company falls under 1.50, plus crash, halt, bankruptcy, appeal and bailout stories in the news and the Discord feed.

SITE

  • NEWA crash banner on the market page, BANKRUPT and DISTRESSED tags on the board, and a bailout countdown in place of the goal on a bankrupt company's page.

Board Meeting

REND-CONOMY

  • CHANGEDDividends have a board now. No more on/off switch. Each company pays its dividend at a rate its board sets: 125%, 100%, 75%, 50%, 25%, or nothing at all. Every 8 to 16 hours the board meets and moves the rate one step toward what the company can actually afford. Healthy companies pay more. Struggling ones get cut.
  • CHANGEDFailed goals cut, they don't freeze. A miss knocks the dividend down a step (two for a big one). Only a company in real trouble gets suspended outright, and it stays suspended until the board decides otherwise.
  • NEWWins fix it. A successful goal, or a good rumor that turns out true, brings a cut dividend straight back to 100%. A big success boosts it to 150% for a few hours, on top of the usual bonus.
  • NEWSpecial dividends. Good news sometimes comes with a one-off payout of one to three days' worth. Collect it with r!dividends like any other.
  • NEWBoard decisions show up in the market news and the Discord feed, and the company pages and the bot's dividend line show each company's current rate.
  • FIXEDAll five companies could end up frozen at the same time. That was most of the problem.

The Market, Rewired

REND-CONOMY MARKET

  • CHANGEDA brand new market engine. Prices no longer drift back to where they started. Companies go through good stretches and bad ones, calm days and wild ones, and none of it lasts forever. Big moves make more big moves for a while, then things settle. The whole market has moods too.
  • CHANGEDGoal results can't be read off the chart anymore. Whether a company hits its goal comes down to how it's really doing behind the scenes. The price sometimes moves before the deadline, and it isn't always right. Goals can now end in a mixed result too, and some wins and misses are bigger than others.
  • NEWBreaking news lands with no warning and moves the price on the spot.
  • NEWRumors. Whispers about a company nudge its price. Hours later they're confirmed or denied. Most turn out true. Most.
  • NEWMarket-wide stories hit every company at once, and crossover stories put two companies in the same headline.
  • CHANGEDBastardly Glory has been reined in. No more 400 one day and 2 the next. It's still the wildest stock on the board, with huge swings, hype that builds before every goal, and a bad habit of selling off even after good news. Its new board expects the price to come back down to Earth.
