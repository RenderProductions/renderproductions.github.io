THE MUSEUM

What every collectible is worth to collectors today, and what's on display in your servers.

A collectible's collector value follows how many exist across every server: rarer than the average collectible, it's worth more (up to 5× Marquez's price); more common, less (down to half). It's worked out once a day and counts toward your net worth. r!collection shows yours.

How the museum works

  • Donate a collectible with r!museum donate <collectible>. It's gone for good, and the museum pays you 75% of its collector value. The first of each kind puts your name on the plaque.
  • Every kind on display gives everyone in that server +0.5% chat pay and shift pay.
  • The wings: the Founders' Wing (every weekly special) gives +5% property rent; the Winter Wing (the holiday collectibles) doubles the Polygons behind advent doors; the Birthday Wing (the Party Hat and every Candle so far) makes the birthday cake 20% smaller; The Full Collection adds +2% to every achievement reward.
  • Mr. Glaz buys collectibles at night for 125% of their value (r!ug sell <collectible>): one a night, only ones you've had for a week, and it adds Heat. Whatever he buys is never seen again.
  • Collectibles are never loan collateral.
  • Museum Donor (rare): surrender your most prized possession to the museum. The Curator (mythic): surrender all of your most valued items to the Museum.
